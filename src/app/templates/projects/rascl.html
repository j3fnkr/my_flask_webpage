It is offered by the Institute for Radio Frequency Engineering and Electronics (IRS), particularly the chairs of Regelungs- und Steuerungssysteme (RUS) and Automatisierungstechnik (VSA). Within a complex automation task, teams of three design and build an automation concept end-to-end, covering hardware design, communication over an industrial bus system, drive system control and more, working with laboratory-scale robotic manipulator arms. {% endblock %} {% block description %}

Teaching Content

This module is designed to teach students the stated qualification objectives in the fields of automation, robotics and control by means of a complex automation task. The task not only covers the automation concept itself but also underlying aspects like hardware design, communication using an industrial bus system, drive system control and others. To do so, laboratory systems, namely laboratory scale robotic manipulator arms, are provided to the students.

Examination

The examination consists of pitches after every thematic block, a final presentation and an oral examination of 20 minutes. Additionally, the produced code and CAD models are evaluated. The module grade results from the assessment of the pitches, the presentation, the oral exam and the code and CAD quality.

Enrollment

Due to the limited number of 27 places, students must apply to participate.

Recommended Background

Knowledge in automation, control and robotics in general. Recommended, but not mandatory, lectures are:

  • Practical Tools for Control Engineers
  • Cyber Physical Modeling
  • Seminar Industrial Process and Plant Engineering
  • Optimal Control
  • Multivariable Control Systems
  • Nonlinear Control Systems
