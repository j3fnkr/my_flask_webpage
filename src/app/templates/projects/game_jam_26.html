About the Global Game Jam 2026

The global game jam (GGJ) is an event were people around the world meet up in different sites to plan, design and create a game and submit it before the deadline. The GGJ team publishes a theme every year, which should be included in any way into your game idea. The theme of 2026th game jam was "mask".

My Team

Our team consisted of three jammers, two friends of mine and me. They already participated in the 2025 game and so they were more experienced than me. It was my first jam event and I did not ever use any kind of game engine until the first day of the jam.

The Location

We participated in the GODOT Stammtisch e. v. game site in Karlsruhe. It was very well organized and breakfast, pizza and snacks were provided with much love. This was the perfect environment for a two day long jam event to fully focus on the task to finish the game. {% endblock %} {% block docu %}

The design process of Calavera

{{ render_image('img/ggj26/calavera.png', alt='Calavera game field', link='https://pebll.itch.io/calavera', link_text='calavera @ itch.io') }}

On Friday night we installed the godot game engine and brainstormed about different ideas for a game related to the mask topic. After agreeing on the game idea, we distributed roles - art, music, game logic. We went to bed early to continue fresh on another day.

Saturday was the main implementation day. We started early with breakfast on the game jam site. We had the deadline at midnight to submit a sneak preview video to the jam site's organizers. I mainly worked on the game logic. This was the milestone we set for the day - still we missed the deadline by over 15 minutes.

For Sunday we knew that we could not go beyond the deadline again, so we wrapped up what was still missing and what we definitely wanted implemented. This was the main focus. Every minute counted. In the late afternoon, I focused on the implementation of sound effects and the background music. We recorded three pieces by ourselves (two on guitar and one with the violin), which we wanted to use as background music. This time we made the deadline even though it was close again.
