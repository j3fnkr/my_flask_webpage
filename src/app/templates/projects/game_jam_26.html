About the Global Game Jam 2026

The Global Game Jam (GGJ) is an event where people around the world meet up at various sites to plan, design and create a game, and submit it before the deadline. The GGJ team publishes a theme every year, which should be incorporated into your game idea in some way. The theme of the 2026 game jam was "mask".

My Team

Our team consisted of three jammers - two of my friends and me. They had already participated in the 2025 game jam, so they were more experienced than me. It was my first jam event and I had never used a game engine before the first day of the jam.

The Location

We took part in the jam at the GODOT Stammtisch e. v. site in Karlsruhe. It was very well organized, and breakfast, pizza and snacks were provided with much love. This was the perfect environment for a two-day-long jam event. We could fully focus on the task of finishing the game.

The design process of Calavera

{{ render_image('img/ggj26/calavera.png', alt='Calavera game field', link='https://pebll.itch.io/calavera', link_text='calavera @ itch.io') }}

On Friday night we installed the Godot game engine and brainstormed ideas for a game around the mask theme. After agreeing on the game idea, we assigned the roles - art, music and game logic. We went to bed early to start fresh the next day.

Saturday was the main implementation day. We started early with breakfast at the jam site. We had a midnight deadline to submit a sneak preview video to the jam site's organizers. I mainly worked on the game logic. This was the milestone we had set for the day - yet we missed the deadline by over 15 minutes.

On Sunday, we knew we could not miss the deadline again, so we wrapped up what was still missing and what we definitely wanted to see implemented. This was our main focus. Every minute counted. In the late afternoon, I focused on implementing the sound effects and the background music. We recorded three pieces ourselves (two on guitar, one on violin), which we wanted to use as background music. This time we made the deadline, even though it was close again.
